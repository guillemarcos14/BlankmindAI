## Quality and decision gates

Functional: completed nonempty reply, exact authorized action when requested, no action otherwise, durable forgetting; no invented physical success. Facts: exact metric, date bounds, units and provenance, known zero versus missing data, no resurrected forgotten records. Presentation: deterministic Unicode citation/source UUID/internal-label scan on visible drafts and final text, with explicit quoted-input exceptions. Model reviewer evaluates all 80 eligible cases plus 24 negative cases (scenario indices 0,4,6,9,12,14,17,18,20,22,25,39, each ES/EN), and all functional failures. Every turn receives deterministic review; unsampled semantic quality is explicitly unjudged by model. Reviewer is gpt-5.6-sol, $4 input/$0.40 cache/$20 output per million (official model page, rechecked Oct8), within the shared 4 USD budget. Single reviewer attempt, no replacement of failed reviews. Model review never substitutes for human judgement.

Continue narrowly only if no safety/authority regressions, no poor accelerated factual answers, no candidate-exclusive critical failures, and eligible p50/p95 first/final improve >=20%, known complete cost improves >=20%. Noneligible >10% p50 or >20% p95 slowdown blocks expansion beyond narrow eligible testing. Any missing metering prevents exact complete cost-gate closure. Human review required before activation: 24 blinded pairs, balanced language/routes and difficult profiles/context, score accuracy, provenance, usefulness/naturalness and choose A/B/tie. Frozen human case IDs: wide-1,5,12,16,23,27,34,38,45,49,56,60,67,61,78,72,89,90,109,110,121,122,159,160. No activation based solely on a model verdict.
