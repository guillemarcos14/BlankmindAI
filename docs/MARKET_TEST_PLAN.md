# Blank Market Test Plan

## Test Goal

Validate whether people will use and pay for a physical NFC-based app blocker.

## Target Segments

- Students who lose focus to social apps.
- Knowledge workers who want deep work sessions.
- People trying to reduce late-night phone use.
- ADHD/productivity communities where physical commitment devices may resonate.

## Founder Base

Founders are the users who signed up before Blank was created or publicly launched.

There is only one founder group. The goal is to thank them for their early trust, not to split them into separate beta/founding tiers.

Founder communication should offer:

- 24-hour priority access before the public launch.
- Clear thanks for joining before the product existed.
- 3-day preferred stock reservation window.
- 50% discount on the first Blank Physical Device with code `Founders`.

## Beta Cohort

If a practical product test is needed, recruit from:

- Personal network.
- Productivity and digital wellbeing communities.
- University groups.
- Indie hacker/startup communities.

Tester requirements:


- iPhone users can join iOS beta only if Family Controls entitlement/TestFlight path is available.
- Willing to use one physical NFC tag for at least 7 days.

## Offer Tests

Test three positions:

- Focus: "Start a focus session by tapping a physical tag."
- Addiction reduction: "Make distracting apps harder to access."
- Sleep: "Lock social apps away from the bed."

Price tests:

- 12.99 EUR launch price for the Blank Physical Device.
- Gradual pricing increases after the first launch phase.
- 1.99 EUR/month future subscription, not implemented yet.

## Success Criteria

Strong signal:

- 30 percent or higher visitor-to-waitlist conversion on targeted traffic.
- 10 percent or higher waitlist-to-paid preorder.
- 60 percent or higher beta setup completion.
- 40 percent or higher Day 7 active usage.
- 5 or more testers say they would be disappointed if Blank disappeared.

Weak signal:

- Users like the idea but do not finish setup.
- Users use it once and do not repeat.
- Users want software-only scheduling instead of physical NFC control.

## Interview Questions

- What app or behavior did you want Blank to help with?
- When did you use it?
- What made setup confusing?
- Did the physical tag make the commitment feel stronger?
- What would make this worth paying for?
- What would stop you from recommending it?
- What should happen if you lose the tag?

## Feedback Form Fields

- Device model.
- iOS version.
- Did setup complete?
- Apps blocked.
- Number of Blank sessions used.
- Biggest frustration.
- Did it help you focus?
- Would you pay 12.99 EUR for the Blank Physical Device?
- Would 1.99 EUR/month feel fair for a future subscription if Blank keeps helping you reduce app use?
- Email for follow-up.
