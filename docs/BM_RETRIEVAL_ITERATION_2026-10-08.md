Status: evaluation in progress; default off, no deployment.

- Shared repaired baseline 272008d: complete persisted protection reads report zero recorded minutes separately from physical coverage; generic planner uses local Monday calendar weeks and all eligible measurements. Candidate inherits both fixes so they do not masquerade as classifier benefits.
- Development pilot: old exposed 20 questions, 40 actual turns, runtime 8dfef6f. Retain every error and all original evidence. Pilot may inform routing thresholds; no pilot success certifies generalization.
- Final comparison: 40 fresh authored questions, 80 actual matched AB/BA turns. New wording, measured sleep zero, differing durations and unioned protection values; missing and forgotten sleep, empty protection. Manifest authored before first execution. No independently sealed human holdout.
- Threshold adjustment fixed after exposed pilot, before fresh cases. Actual separate negative classifier checks include exposed and new cases; confidence is not certified precision.
- Both variants use gpt-5.6-luna, identical fixtures, context, source ownership and durable endpoint. Candidate adds Decisions gpt-6-luna and compact final-only prose schema; server controls all action and memory fields. Thus benefits measure the complete architecture, not classifier alone.

- Timing starts at local handler input and ends at durable completion; provisional draft resets are respected. Real providers and private QA DB. Deployed transport, device and voice not measured.
- Gate: all final candidate responses acceptable or excellent according to a separate model, no paired new poor answers, no errors increase, both medians and p95 improve, lower complete estimated provider cost, classifier guards and cleanup pass. Also disclose count excellent; acceptable does not equal excellence. No activation from this synthetic gate alone.
